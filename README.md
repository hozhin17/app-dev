# My Favorite Series 🎬

## 📺 The Office
The Office is one of my favorite series because of its hilarious moments, unique characters, and documentary-style storytelling.  
I really enjoy the funny yet heartfelt relationships between the employees of Dunder Mifflin.

### **Main Characters**
- Michael Scott
- Jim Halpert
- Pam Beesly
- Dwight Schrute
- Andy Bernard

### **What I Like About It**
- The awkward humor and funny office moments  
- The romantic story between Jim and Pam  
- Iconic quotes and scenes that never get old  

> “I am Beyoncé, always.” — Michael Scott
